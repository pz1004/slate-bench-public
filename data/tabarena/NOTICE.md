the TabArena runs need no download.

The Hugging Face dataset was no longer publicly accessible on 2026-10-08. The copy of
`df_results.csv` here is the unmodified file, with the SHA-256 above, that every TabArena
run used.

Cite TabArena (Erickson et al., 2025, "TabArena: A Living Benchmark for Machine Learning
on Tabular Data") when you reuse these files or results derived from them.
